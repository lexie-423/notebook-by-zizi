### 1. endeavor (v./n. 努力，尝试)

这个词用作名词或动词，表示（尤指新的或困难的）努力、尝试。

* **As a verb (动词):** Governments worldwide must *endeavor* to create sustainable policies to combat climate change.
    (世界各国政府必须努力制定可持续政策以应对气候变化。)
* **As a noun (名词):** Scientific research is a collective *endeavor* that often requires international collaboration.
    (科学研究是一项集体努力，通常需要国际合作。)

### 2. endow (v. 赋予，捐赠)

这个词常用于被动语态 (be endowed with)，表示“天生具有某种品质或才能”。

* It is often argued that humans are *endowed* with the unique ability of abstract thought and complex language.
    (人们常说，人类被赋予了抽象思维和复杂语言的独特能力。)
* Higher education should *endow* students with not only specialized knowledge but also critical thinking skills.
    (高等教育不仅应赋予学生专业知识，还应赋予他们批判性思维能力。)

### 3. entail (v. 包含，使...成为必要)

这个词表示某事物作为必然的结果或组成部分而包含（某事物）。

* Implementing renewable energy solutions on a national scale *entails* substantial initial investment and political will.
    (在全国范围内实施可再生能源解决方案，需要大量的初期投资和政治意愿。)
* A career in medicine *entails* years of rigorous training and a lifelong commitment to learning.
    (从事医疗事业意味着需要经过多年的严格培训和终身学习的承诺。)

### 4. enterprise (n. 事业，企业，进取心)

这个词可以指“公司/企业”，也可以指“（有难度或风险的）事业”或“进取心”。

* **As 'company' (企业):** Small and medium-sized *enterprises* (SMEs) are often considered the backbone of a nation's economy.
    (中小企业通常被视为国家经济的支柱。)
* **As 'initiative' (进取心/事业):** The transition to a green economy will require significant innovation and private *enterprise*.
    (向绿色经济转型需要大量的创新和民间进取精神。)

### 5. entity (n. 实体，独立存在体)

指独立存在的事物，尤指区别于其环境的存在。

* Multinational corporations are powerful economic *entities* that can significantly influence local governments.
    (跨国公司是强大的经济实体，能够极大地影响地方政府。)
* While the European Union acts as a single economic bloc, each member state remains a sovereign *entity*.
    (尽管欧盟是一个单一经济集团，但每个成员国仍然是主权实体。)

### 6. envisage (v. 设想，展望，正视)

比 'imagine' 更正式，指“展望”或“设想”未来可能发生的事。

* It is *envisaged* that artificial intelligence will fundamentally transform the labor market within the next decade.
    (据设想，人工智能将在未来十年内从根本上改变劳动力市场。)
* The urban planners *envisage* a city where public transport is fully electric and autonomous.
    (城市规划者设想未来城市中的公共交通将完全实现电动化和自动化。)

### 7. escalate (v. （使）逐步升级，恶化)

指（战斗、冲突、问题等）的逐步升级或加剧。

* Without immediate intervention, minor urban problems can *escalate* into major social crises.
    (如果不立即干预，轻微的城市问题可能会升级为重大的社会危机。)
* The problem of plastic pollution in oceans has *escalated* dramatically, threatening marine ecosystems worldwide.
    (海洋中的塑料污染问题急剧恶化，威胁着全球的海洋生态系统。)

### 8. escort (v./n. 护送，陪同)

*注：这个词在雅思议论文中不那么常用，但在描述社会现象或安全问题时可能用到。*

* **As a noun (名词):** For security reasons, high-profile delegates attending the summit were given a police *escort*.
    (出于安全原因，参加峰会的高调代表都配备了警方护送。)
* **As a verb (动词):** The museum guide *escorted* the visitors through the exhibition, explaining the history of the artifacts.
    (博物馆导游陪同游客参观展览，讲解文物的历史。)

### 9. esteem (n. 尊重，尊敬)

常用于 "self-esteem" (自尊) 或 "be held in high esteem" (备受尊敬)。

* Professions in education and healthcare are often held in high *esteem* by society.
    (教育和医疗行业的职业通常受到社会的高度尊重。)
* Developing a strong sense of self-*esteem* is crucial for a child's psychological development and future success.
    (培养强烈的自尊对儿童的心理发展和未来成功至关重要。)

### 10. evade (v. 规避，逃避)

指巧妙地（或非法地）避开某事物，尤其是责任、问题或法律。

* Some multinational corporations utilize complex legal loopholes to *evade* paying their fair share of taxes.
    (一些跨国公司利用复杂的法律漏洞来逃避缴纳其应付的税款。)
* Individuals should not attempt to *evade* their responsibility to protect the natural environment.
    (个人不应试图逃避保护自然环境的责任。)

### 11. excerpt (n. 摘录，选段)

*注：这个词在学术讨论中常用，但在雅思大作文论证中相对少见，除非是引用数据。*

* This *excerpt* from a recent study on climate change indicates that sea levels are rising faster than previously predicted.
    (这份近期气候变化研究的摘录表明，海平面上升速度快于此前的预测。)
* The professor included an *excerpt* from a famous speech to illustrate the point.
    (教授引用了一段著名演讲的节选来阐明观点。)

### 12. excursion (n. 短途旅行，远足)

常用于讨论旅游业或教育话题。

* Educational *excursions* to museums, art galleries, or historical sites are a valuable component of a well-rounded education.
    (去博物馆、美术馆或历史古迹的教育性短途旅行是全面教育的重要组成部分。)
* The rise of ecotourism has led to more organized *excursions* into protected natural areas.
    (生态旅游的兴起导致了更多有组织的进入自然保护区的短途旅行。)

### 13. execute (v. 执行，实施；处决)

*(注：您输入的是 excute，正确的拼写是 **execute**。)*

* **As 'carry out' (执行):** For any new government policy to be effective, it must be carefully planned and efficiently *executed*.
    (一项新的政府政策要生效，就必须仔细规划并有效执行。)
* **As 'death penalty' (处决):** The ethical debate over whether states should *execute* criminals remains highly controversial.
    (关于国家是否应该处决罪犯的伦理辩论仍然极具争议。)

### 14. exert (v. 施加，运用)

常用于 "exert influence/pressure on..." (对...施加影响/压力)。

* Advertisements *exert* a powerful influence on consumer behavior, particularly on young people.
    (广告对消费者行为施加了强大的影响，尤其是对年轻人。)
* Human industrial activities *exert* immense pressure on the planet's fragile ecosystems.
    (人类的工业活动对地球脆弱的生态系统施加了巨大的压力。)

### 15. expel (v. 驱逐，开除)

* Schools must have clear policies regarding the circumstances under which they will *expel* a student for serious misconduct.
    (学校必须有明确的政策，规定在何种情况下会因严重不当行为开除学生。)
* Governments reserve the right to *expel* foreign nationals who pose a threat to national security.
    (政府保留驱逐对国家安全构成威胁的外国公民的权利。)

### 16. expenditure (n. 支出，花费)

在讨论政府预算、家庭开支等话题时非常常用。

* Government *expenditure* on healthcare and education is widely seen as a crucial investment in a nation's future.
    (政府在医疗和教育上的支出被广泛视为对国家未来的关键投资。)
* In many major cities, housing accounts for the largest proportion of household *expenditure*.
    (在许多大城市，住房占家庭开支的最大比例。)
