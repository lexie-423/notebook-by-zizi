### 1. furnish (v. 供应，提供)

这个词比 'provide' 更正式，常用于提供必要或期望的东西（如信息、设备等）。

* Universities have a responsibility to *furnish* students with the resources necessary for their research, including access to comprehensive libraries and laboratories.
    (大学有责任为学生提供研究所需的资源，包括使用综合图书馆和实验室的权限。)
* To make an informed decision, the public must be *furnished* with accurate and unbiased information regarding the new policy.
    (为了做出明智的决定，必须向公众提供有关新政策的准确和公正的信息。)

### 2. fuse (v. （使）融合，合并)

指将不同的事物（如思想、风格、材料）结合在一起，形成一个整体。

* Modern architecture often attempts to *fuse* innovative design with principles of environmental sustainability.
    (现代建筑经常试图将创新设计与环境可持续性原则融为一体。)
* The cultural identity of a nation is often the result of different traditions and beliefs *fusing* over centuries.
    (一个国家的文化认同通常是不同传统和信仰历经数百年融合的结果。)

### 3. fuss (n. 大惊小怪，无谓的纷扰)

*注：这个词偏向非正式，在雅思大作文中应谨慎使用。用 "controversy", "disagreement" 或 "excessive attention" 替换通常更合适。但在特定语境下可以用来批评某种现象。*

* There is often a great deal of media *fuss* surrounding celebrity lifestyles, which distracts public attention from more pressing global issues.
    (媒体经常对名人生活方式大肆报道，这分散了公众对更紧迫全球问题的注意力。)

### 4. futile (adj. 徒劳的，无效的)

指完全没有成功可能性的，无用的。

* Without genuine international cooperation, isolated attempts by individual countries to solve climate change may prove *futile*.
    (如果没有真正的国际合作，个别国家解决气候变化的孤立尝试可能会被证明是徒劳的。)
* It is *futile* to argue that technology has no impact on social structures; its influence is pervasive and undeniable.
    (争论技术对社会结构没有影响是徒劳的；它的影响是普遍且不可否认的。)

### 5. gear (v. 使...适合，使...配合)

常用于被动语态或 "gear towards" (使...面向...)，表示为特定目的、群体或标准而设计或调整。

* Educational curricula should be *geared towards* fostering critical thinking and adaptability, rather than simply memorizing facts.
    (教育课程应以培养批判性思维和适应能力为导向，而不仅仅是记忆事实。)
* Many government policies are *geared to* boosting economic growth, sometimes at the expense of environmental protection.
    (许多政府政策都旨在促进经济增长，有时却以牺牲环境保护为代价。)

### 6. gauge (v. 估量，判断，测量)

指评估、判断或测量（尤指难以精确测量的东西，如情绪、影响力）。

* It is difficult to accurately *gauge* the long-term impact of artificial intelligence on the job market.
    (很难准确估量人工智能对就业市场的长期影响。)
* Standardized testing is one of the primary methods used to *gauge* academic performance, though its reliability is often debated.
    (标准化考试是用来衡量学业成绩的主要方法之一，尽管其可靠性经常受到争议。)

### 7. gigantic (adj. 巨大的，庞大的)

*注：这是一个强有力的形容词。在学术写作中，'substantial', 'enormous' 或 'significant' 更常见，但 'gigantic' 可以用来强调规模之大。*

* The challenge of managing waste in modern metropolises has become a *gigantic* operational and environmental problem.
    (在现代大都市管理废弃物已成为一个巨大的运营和环境问题。)
* The internet provides access to a *gigantic* amount of information, but not all of it is accurate or reliable.
    (互联网提供了巨大的信息量，但并非所有信息都是准确或可靠的。)

### 8. foremost (adj. 最重要的，首要的)

指在重要性、地位或排名上处于最前面的。

* The *foremost* priority for any government should be the safety and well-being of its citizens.
    (任何政府的首要任务都应该是其公民的安全和福祉。)
* Improving public transportation is one of the *foremost* solutions to reducing urban traffic congestion and pollution.
    (改善公共交通是减少城市交通拥堵和污染的最重要解决方案之一。)

### 9. forge (v. 建立，缔造；伪造)

* **As 'build' (建立):** International cooperation is essential to *forge* lasting solutions to global problems such as pandemics and terrorism.
    (国际合作对于缔造针对流行病和恐怖主义等全球性问题的持久解决方案至关重要。)
* **As 'build' (建立):** Higher education provides an environment where students can *forge* professional connections that benefit their future careers.
    (高等教育提供了一个环境，让学生可以建立有益于他们未来职业生涯的专业联系。)

### 10. formidable (adj. 令人敬畏的，难以对付的)

指因强大、困难或庞大而令人产生敬畏感或恐惧感。

* Transitioning an entire nation to renewable energy sources is a *formidable* task, requiring immense political will and investment.
    (将整个国家转向可再生能源是一项艰巨的任务，需要巨大的政治意愿和投资。)
* Despite *formidable* obstacles, many developing countries have made significant progress in reducing poverty.
    (尽管存在难以克服的障碍，许多发展中国家在减少贫困方面取得了重大进展。)

### 11. fracture (v./n. （使）破裂，分裂)

常用于比喻义，指社会、团体或体系的分裂。

* **As a noun (名词):** The significant income gap between urban and rural areas represents a deep *fracture* in the nation's economic structure.
    (城乡之间巨大的收入差距代表了国家经济结构的深层断裂。)
* **As a verb (动词):** Intense political debate without mutual respect can *fracture* social cohesion and lead to polarization.
    (没有相互尊重的激烈政治辩论会破坏社会凝聚力并导致两极分化。)
